About Wings Academy

Wings Academy is an online tutoring portal that provides a Virtual Learning Environment (VLE) for IGCSE / O/A Level (CAIE, IGCSE AQA, ACCA & Edexcel) and SAT students.

Wings Academy is one of the finest O & A Level academy with the most qualified and experienced teaching staff. We have been providing guidance to students both Local (In-house classes) and International (online classes) Since 2016.

About Manager

Sir Meer Atique Chaudhary (MAC) is an experienced educationist, trainer, and entrepreneur. He specializes in teaching O/A Level Accounting and professional qualifications like ACCA, CA, CIMA, PIPFA, HDA/HND, and ACT, with over a decade of experience.

Currently, he is the Head of O Level Accounting at Lahore Grammar School SKP, where his teaching methods, including online video lectures, notes, and problem solutions, have helped students achieve excellent results. He also previously served as Principal at Resource Academia International School System.

In addition, MAC has 12 years of experience running his own institute, Wings Academy.
